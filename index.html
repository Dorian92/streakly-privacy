Streakly Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Streakly does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data we collect

None. Streakly has no servers and does not send any information off your device. We, the developer, cannot see your habits, your progress or anything else you do in the app.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • the habits you create, their schedules and reminder times;
  • the days you mark as done, your streaks, levels and badges;
  • your app settings, such as theme and reminder preferences.

This data is removed when you delete the app.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by the app on your device as local notifications. They are not sent through any server. On Android, the app may restart your reminders after the phone reboots so they keep working.

Backups

Streakly can save a backup file of your data when you ask it to. You choose where the file goes: a folder on your device, or another app you share it with (for example cloud storage or email). Once you share a backup with another app or service, that app's or service's privacy policy applies to the file. Streakly never creates or uploads backups on its own.

Tracking, ads and third parties

Streakly contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking. It does not use your data for tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Streakly does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control: you can edit or delete habits in the app, turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings, and remove all data by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
